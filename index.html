FermentPad

Keep your pickle, kraut, and canning recipes in one place, and follow every batch from first brine to last jar.

What it does

  • Recipes

    Save your brines and ratios once, with the salt percentage worked out for you. Start a new batch from any recipe in a tap.

  • Batches

    Log photos, tasting notes, and pH or salinity readings as each batch ferments, with a progress bar toward its ready day.

  • Canning and jars

    Record processing time, check your seals, set a best-by date, and count down the jars left on the shelf.

  • Reminders

    Get a nudge when it's time to check a batch, when it should be ready, and when to test the seals.
